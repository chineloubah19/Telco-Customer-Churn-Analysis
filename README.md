# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for telecommunications companies because losing customers can reduce recurring revenue and increase the cost of acquiring new customers.

This project analyzes customer churn patterns to identify high-risk customer segments, understand the services and payment methods associated with churn, and assess the financial impact of customer loss.

The analysis was developed using Microsoft Excel and Power BI to transform customer data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The telecommunications company needs to understand why customers are leaving and which customer groups are most at risk of churn.

This analysis addresses key business questions:

- Which customers are most likely to churn?
- Which contract types have the highest churn?
- How does customer tenure influence churn?
- Which services are associated with higher churn?
- Which payment methods have the highest churn?
- What is the financial impact of customer churn?

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Calculate the overall customer churn and retention rates.
- Identify customer segments with high churn risk.
- Analyze churn across contract types and tenure groups.
- Examine the relationship between services and customer churn.
- Analyze churn across different payment methods.
- Quantify the monthly revenue associated with churned customers.
- Estimate customer lifetime value.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Month-to-Month Customers Have the Highest Churn

Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at **42.71%**, making them the highest-risk contract segment.

### Early-Tenure Customers Are at Higher Risk

Customers with **0–12 months of tenure** have the highest churn rate at **47.68%**, highlighting the importance of early customer engagement and retention.

### Fiber Optic Customers Show High Churn

Fiber optic customers have a churn rate of **41.89%**, making this service category an important area for further investigation.

### Electronic Check Customers Have the Highest Churn

Customers using electronic checks have the highest churn rate among payment methods at **45.29%**.

### Customer Churn Has a Significant Revenue Impact

Churned customers represent approximately **$139.13K in monthly recurring charges**, highlighting the financial importance of improving customer retention.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Strengthen Early Customer Retention

Introduce stronger onboarding and engagement programs during the first 12 months to reduce early-stage churn.

### 2. Encourage Longer-Term Contracts

Provide incentives that encourage month-to-month customers to move to one-year or two-year contracts.

### 3. Investigate Fiber Optic Churn

Review pricing, service quality, customer support and customer expectations among fiber optic users.

### 4. Review Electronic Check Payments

Investigate potential payment friction or customer experience issues associated with electronic check payments.

### 5. Prioritize High-Risk Customers

Use customer characteristics, tenure, contract type and service information to identify customers who may benefit from proactive retention efforts.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- The analysis is based on the variables available in the Telco Customer Churn dataset.
- Monthly Revenue Lost represents the monthly recurring charges associated with churned customers.
- Estimated Customer Lifetime Value is based on available customer tenure and monthly charge information.
- The analysis identifies patterns and associations but does not establish direct causation.
